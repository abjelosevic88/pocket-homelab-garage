# Roadmap

Ideas, roughly in order. Open an issue if one matters to you.

- **Receipts by camera** — snap a receipt on the quick page and attach it to the fill-up or record.
- **Tyre sets** — track summer/winter sets with their own mileage and tread depth; the swap reminder moves the active set.
- **Fuel price lookup** — pull the day's price from a public feed to pre-fill the quick page.
- **OBD / car APIs** — pull the odometer straight from the car where an API exists (Tesla, VW ID, Hyundai Bluelink).
- **Pocket Split** — mark a repair as shared and send it to a group in one click.
- **Shared read-only link** — a public page for a vehicle's history when you sell it.
- **PDF service book** — the full history as a printable booklet.
- **Multi-currency reports** — totals in the home currency across vehicles with different currencies (per-vehicle works today).
- **LubeLogger extra fields** — map custom fields to tags on import instead of folding them into notes.
